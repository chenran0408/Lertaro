# Lertaro 6.1 Beta 7

- The full search window now lets you hide the filter panel when you need more room for results.
- Use the filter button in the title bar or press `Ctrl+Shift+F` to switch the panel on or off.
- The filter button shows a translated tooltip and keeps the selected filters unchanged when the panel is hidden.

这是测试版本。普通电脑选择 `Lertaro-Setup.exe`，ARM 电脑选择 `Lertaro-Setup-arm64.exe`。
