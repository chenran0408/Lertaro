# Lertaro 6.3.2

- The clipboard panel shortcut is now easy to find: it sits in its own "Clipboard Panel" section at the bottom of Settings > Hotkeys, and searching "clipboard" in Settings finds it directly.
- Pasting from the clipboard panel now closes the panel first, so the text always lands in the window you were typing in.
- The advanced mouse-wake settings stay out of the way until you expand them.

普通电脑选择 `Lertaro-Setup.exe`；ARM 电脑选择 `Lertaro-Setup-arm64.exe`。也可以下载对应的免安装版 ZIP。
