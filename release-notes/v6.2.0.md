# Lertaro 6.2

- New clipboard history: text you copy is saved on your computer so you can find it again later.
- Search your clipboard history from the main search box by typing `cb` followed by a keyword.
- The Quick Panel now has a clipboard tab where you can paste, copy, pin, or delete entries in one click.
- Clipboard data stays on this computer only, and passwords copied from password managers such as KeePass or Bitwarden are never recorded.
- Faster and steadier full-window search, plus routine fixes under the hood.

普通电脑选择 `Lertaro-Setup.exe`；ARM 电脑选择 `Lertaro-Setup-arm64.exe`。也可以下载对应的免安装版 ZIP。
